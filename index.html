A letter to my favorite person

My love,

I wish I could hand you these flowers in person, watch your face light up, and tell you everything that sometimes gets caught between my heart and my words. But for now, I made this little corner of the world for you. A place you can come back to whenever you need a reminder that you are loved, appreciated, and held close in someone’s heart.

There is something about you that makes even an ordinary day feel a little softer. It is in your smile, in the way you talk about the things you care about, and in all the little details you probably don’t think anyone notices. I notice. And the more I get to know you, the more reasons I find to be grateful for you.

I love the thought of us sharing the simplest things: a conversation that lasts longer than we planned, a quiet walk, a silly joke, or just being beside each other without having to fill every silence. I don’t need every moment to be extraordinary. With you, even the small ones are worth keeping.

If you could see yourself through my eyes, I hope you would be a little kinder to yourself. You would see someone who doesn’t have to be perfect to be wonderful. Someone whose feelings matter, whose dreams deserve room to grow, and whose presence brings a kind of warmth that cannot be replaced.

On the days when everything feels heavy, I hope you remember that you don’t have to have all the answers. You are allowed to rest. You are allowed to be quiet, to feel unsure, and to take your time. You don’t need to hide the difficult parts of your day to deserve gentleness. I want to listen to those parts, too.

I cannot promise that I will always find the perfect words, or that I will never get things wrong. But I want to keep learning how to love you with patience, honesty, and care. I want to understand you better, listen when something matters to you, and show you through my actions that your heart is safe to be itself with me.

Thank you for being you. For the little moments that stay in my mind long after they happen. For the happiness that comes with seeing your name, hearing your voice, or simply knowing that you are part of my life. You may not realize how much those things mean to me, but they do. More than I can fit into one letter.

This bouquet may be made of lines and colors, but the feeling behind it is real. Every flower is a little way of saying what I hope you never forget: you matter to me. You are someone I want to make time for, someone I want to celebrate, and someone I want to keep choosing in the quiet, everyday ways that love becomes something lasting.

So take these flowers, and take your time with this letter. There is no hurry here. Just a little piece of my heart, waiting for you whenever you need it. If this makes you smile, even just a little, then it has done exactly what I hoped it would.

Until I can give you a real bouquet, let this one bloom for you. And when you reach the end of these words, know that my care for you keeps going.

With all my love,
Always yours.
